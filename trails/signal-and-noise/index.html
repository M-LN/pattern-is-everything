Signal in the noise

Every field has the same problem: real structure mixed with randomness. These stops are the tools for pulling them apart — and for checking you have not invented a pattern that was never there.

Start the trail →
  1. 1 Pattern Essays Signal in the Noise The hard part is rarely finding a pattern. It is resisting the ones that are only noise.
  2. 2 Timeseries Engineering Decomposition Split a series into trend, season and residual. What is left after the explained parts is your noise.
  3. 3 Technical Indicators Simple Moving Average The simplest filter there is: average the last N points and the random wiggles partly cancel out.
  4. 4 Timeseries Engineering Exponential Smoothing Fade the past instead of cutting it off — the forecaster's version of the EMA traders use.
  5. 5 Pattern Essays The Deep Kalman Filter Predict, then correct with each new measurement, weighting each by how noisy you know it to be.
  6. 6 ML Math Bias-Variance Tradeoff Smooth too much and you miss the signal; too little and you fit the noise. The same tradeoff, in model form.
  7. 7 The Toolkit Walk-Forward Validation The final test: does the pattern hold on data it has never seen, in the order it arrives?
The pattern

Filtering, smoothing and regularizing are one move: decide how much to trust each new observation. Out-of-sample testing is how you find out whether you decided well.
