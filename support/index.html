The Mission

Pattern is Everything is a free, open-source educational platform — 249 core interactive topics across Machine Learning & Markets. No ads. No paywalls. No tracking cookies. Just patterns, made visual.

We believe learning should be accessible to everyone. Every diagram, formula, and code snippet on this site will always be free.

100%
of donations go to mental health

Every contribution you make goes directly to mental health organizations. We keep nothing. The site runs on zero cost — no servers to pay for, no team to fund. This is a passion project, and your generosity goes where it matters.

See donation log →

Where It Goes

When donations come in, they will be forwarded to organizations working on mental health support and awareness. We believe that understanding patterns — in data, in language, in markets — starts with understanding ourselves.

The recipient will be confirmed before the first transfer and published in the donation log, including the amount, date, and organization receiving it.
